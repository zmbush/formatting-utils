Welcome to Ellipsus! We’re so glad you’re here! This is a short guide covering some important details—getting started with documents, drafting, formatting, organizing your work, sharing, and more. For more information on features, visit the knowledge base . (If you’d like to learn about using Ellipsus on your phone, see our mobile guide .) Getting started ✍️ You can start writing in Ellipsus in three ways: Create a new document Create from a template Import documents from markdown or .docx You can create documents from your dashboard . From the dash, you can: Access My work , where all your documents and folders live View documents that have been shared with you, under Shared Organize your projects with folders Search for your documents Visit your settings page to set a password , profile picture , or choose a theme Once you’re familiar with your dash, it’s time to open a document and start exploring. Understanding documents and drafts Right now, you're looking at a main document. But writing is rarely a straight line. That’s where drafts come in. In Ellipsus, every document has one main document and as many drafts as you need. A draft is a standalone copy of your document. You can use drafts to: Collect feedback from betas or editors Tinker with a new version of a scene Store research or notes Try something new—without affecting the main document And (much) more! Learn about how drafts work . Formatting your document Your main formatting tools live in the Format panel on the right (click the T icon to open it), and in the floating bottom bar. From here, you can: Select fonts and text sizes Style your text Create headers to structure your doc (which automatically builds your document outline ) Add color or highlights to text Insert images into your document For more: How to format your document Using outlines Using spell check Sharing, collaborating, and permissions Ellipsus supports several roles to keep collaboration clear and intentional: Document owner Can merge role Can edit role Can comment role View-only role And link sharing Find out about Ellipsus’ sharing and permissions here . Ways to collaborate with others include real-time collaboration, comments and chat . You can also read more on how to export your work , or share a snippet or Reader link .
